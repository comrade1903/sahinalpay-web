# Fonts shipped with this site

All three families are redistributable, and are served from this origin rather
than from a third party — see `src/fonts.css` for why. The files here are
subsets generated by `scripts/generate-fonts.py`; the upstream sources and
their licence terms are unchanged.

## Literata (`literata-*.woff2`)

SIL Open Font License 1.1. Copyright 2018 The Literata Project Authors
(https://github.com/googlefonts/literata).
Upstream: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/literata

## Nunito Sans (`nunito-sans-*.woff2`)

SIL Open Font License 1.1. Copyright 2016 The Nunito Sans Project Authors
(https://github.com/googlefonts/NunitoSans).
Upstream: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/nunitosans

The OFL permits redistribution, modification and subsetting. It requires that
the fonts not be sold on their own and that Reserved Font Names not be used by
modified versions — neither applies here: these are subsets served as web
fonts under their original names.

## Material Symbols Outlined (`material-symbols.woff2`)

Apache License 2.0. Copyright Google LLC.
Upstream: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons

Subset to the 24 ligatures this site renders; the full variable font is
roughly 4 MB.
